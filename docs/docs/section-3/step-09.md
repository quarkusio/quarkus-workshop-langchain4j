# Step 09 - Testing and Evaluation

## Evaluating LLM outputs with similarity scoring and model-based judging

A convincing sales recommendation can still be poor advice, even when the equipment is available from a partner. Miles of Smiles needs repeatable checks that upsells suit the trip and respect the customer's preferences. Comparing a winter mountain trip with a summer beach holiday can expose irrelevant snow-chain suggestions, while a family bringing a suitable child seat should not be charged for renting another one.

Similarity scoring and model-based judging help assess whether generated recommendations are relevant and well explained. Ordinary assertions can check seat specifications against supplied child and vehicle details, verify price calculations, and require approval before reservation. A missing measurement should lead to a request for details, and the need for a suitable restraint must not be presented as an obligation to rent from Miles of Smiles.
