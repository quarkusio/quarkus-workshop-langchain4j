# Conclusion: Enterprise Agentic Patterns

The first four steps bring the Miles of Smiles trip planner from prompt-based recommendations to a plan the customer can review and approve after an application restart. Skills supply travel guidance, guardrails check selected rules, and the approval workflow separates plan generation from the customer's later decision.

The remaining chapters are planned exercises on reviewing and revising plans, connecting external services and partner agents, and evaluating recommendation quality. They are not yet complete, so this section does not currently demonstrate the full booking journey.
